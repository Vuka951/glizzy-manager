Coach wanted. No experience needed. Strong stomach required.

Glizzy Manager. Pick one of sixteen characters and take them through the league.

The match is simple. Hide the glizzy under a hat, a sock or a box. The other one guesses. Three grabs, you eat it, you lose.

You don't play. You prepare. Three months before each cup, one move a month: train, rest, or media for cash. Money goes on security, investments and sabotage. And watch the card: ego over seventy-five, and they play on their own.

Four cups a year, straight into a bracket of sixteen. A title, twenty points. A win, three. A loss, minus one. First to three hundred is Glizzy Overlord.

There are sponsors, who double as parties. The Assembly. Elections. Favors. You'll figure it out.

Glizzy Rivals: same league, multiplayer. Up to eight coaches, one room code. Everyone's off-season runs at once, you watch the cup together, bet, and sabotage each other.

Out-eat your friends.
